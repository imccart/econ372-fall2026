Schedule

Below is the plan for each class day. links to the online book chapter and to that day’s slides (a few days draw on two of each). Most days are lectures, and the Activity column is usually blank. It flags the days that carry a graded event, namely a panel discussion (), a simulation (), or the midterm. A short auto-graded quiz () or a homework due date () appears alongside and links to the assignment.

On lecture days we develop the model at the board and work an example. On panel days a randomly drawn panel discusses the reading while the rest of the class earns points asking questions. A simulation is an in-class game followed by a debrief. The longer games fill the class, while the shorter ones run alongside that day’s lecture. Check-in, the panel draw, quizzes, and the question queue all run through our class platform (Commons).

The schedule is provisional — topics, readings, and the exact placement of quizzes and simulations may shift as the term unfolds. Optional material we won’t cover in class is listed under Bonus at the bottom.
