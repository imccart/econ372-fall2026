Schedule

The course runs in four modules, mirroring the path a person takes through the health care system: insurance, physicians, hospitals, and pharmaceuticals.

Class days come in a few recurring formats:

  • Q&A days, where a randomly drawn panel discusses the assigned material and the rest of the class asks questions.
  • Simulation days, where you play both sides of a health care market in a short in-class game and then debrief.
  • Quiz days, where a short, auto-graded quiz opens at the start of class (then a normal Q&A day).
  • The midterm, taken in class.

Check-in, the Q&A panel draw, quizzes, and the question queue all run through our class platform (Commons).

The schedule below is provisional — topics, readings, and the exact placement of quizzes and simulations may shift as the term unfolds.
